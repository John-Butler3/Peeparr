# Common questions

## I already have a media server. Why would I want this?

Peeparr doesn't replace it. It doesn't move, rename or own your files: they
stay where your downloads land, so Plex, Jellyfin or anything else can keep
indexing the same folders.

What Peeparr adds for an adult library:

- **Scenes that are actually recognised.** General media servers are built
  to match movies and TV shows. Adult scenes have no consistent file naming
  and the big movie databases leave them out, so matching takes extra
  plugins and renaming. Peeparr uses Stash's matching and StashDB or
  ThePornDB, which are made for scenes, performers and sites.
- **Kept separate.** It's its own app with its own login and no cloud
  account, so this library never shows up next to the family films. A
  discreet logo can stand in on the home screen.

## Isn't Stash enough on its own?

Stash stays your library: keep using it to scan, tag and organise. Peeparr
sits on top and adds what Stash doesn't do:

- **Finding and downloading new scenes**, and following each one until it's
  in your library.
- **Watching, especially on a phone**: previews that play as you scroll, a
  floating navigation bar, swipe down to a miniplayer, lock-screen controls,
  and an app-like home-screen icon.

## Can't Whisparr do this?

Whisparr does the downloading. Peeparr makes the rest easy: browse the
catalog as pages of scenes, request with one click, see each scene's status
(requested, downloading, importing, failed, ready) beside its title, then
play it once it's in your library.

## Which Whisparr do I need?

Either. Peeparr detects which one you run:

- **Eros (v3)** works with StashDB or ThePornDB as the catalog.
- **v2** works with ThePornDB as the catalog.

There's no second Whisparr to set up.

## Do I have to give anything up?

No. Peeparr is an add-on:

- **Stash** stays your library and does the organising.
- **Whisparr** stays your downloader.
- **Your media server** can carry on alongside, on the same files.

Turn Peeparr off and everything else carries on as before.

## What isn't it?

- Not a general media server: it's for the adult library in Stash, not your
  films and TV.
- Not multi-user: there's one local admin account.
- Not standalone: it needs Stash and Whisparr running.
