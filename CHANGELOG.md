## [1.0.0](https://github.com/John-Butler3/Peeparr/releases/tag/v1.0.0) (2026-10-15)

The first release as **Peeparr**: a self-hosted central hub for your adult library.

### Highlights

* **New look:** a redesigned app around your library, with a glass-style phone navigation bar, smooth page changes and a launch animation.
* **Home:** a banner of the newest scenes in your library with previews (and a soft glow on wide screens), Pick up where you left off, Fresh in your library and Popular right now (ThePornDB's own popular list with a saved website login).
* **Previews everywhere:** hover a scene, or just stop scrolling on a phone, and its Stash preview plays.
* **Discover and request:** Popular, Latest and Oldest on the Scenes page, one-tap requests, and each scene's status (requested, downloading, importing, failed) right beside its title.
* **Watching:** scene pages that resume where you stopped, a miniplayer that carries on as you browse and returns to the scene, swipe down to minimise, lock-screen controls, series played in order, and Up next with related library scenes (even for scenes you don't have yet).
* **Performers and studios:** a photo-collage banner, headers that dock as you scroll, Favorite and Monitor (new scenes requested automatically), studio logos on a matching background.
* **Search:** performers, sites and scenes from one box, a few of each with Show more.
* **Setup:** a step-by-step guide for ThePornDB as the catalog, including the Whisparr-Eros command, ready to copy.
