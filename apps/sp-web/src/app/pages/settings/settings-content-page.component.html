Browsing

Hide non-library ManyVids and FansDB results

When enabled, scenes sourced from amateur/creator platforms (ManyVids, FansDB) are hidden from Scenes, Performer, and Site browsing unless you've already imported them into your library.

Watching

Stream scenes from Pornhub

Scenes that are Pornhub videos (from creators' Pornhub channels) can be watched in Peeparr without being in your library, in Pornhub's own player. They work like your own scenes (the miniplayer, swiping), though play and pause are in Pornhub's player.

This device

Discreet logo

Shows a plain logo -- a dark tile with glowing almond rings -- in the top bar, on the sign-in page, at launch and on the browser tab, on this device only.

It's also the icon when you add Peeparr to your home screen. Already added? Remove it from your home screen and add it again to change the icon.

Default request options
